Make clips

Paste a video link, or upload a video file. The app finds the best moments and cuts up to 6 vertical clips with captions.

Long video or livestream? Clip one part of it:

Minutes to clip (max 30 per job). Leave the start empty to begin from the start. Short videos need nothing here.

Or upload a video file instead (up to 500 MB, at least 1 minute long).

Note: a YouTube link is downloaded in your browser first (your home connection is not blocked by YouTube), then uploaded here for clipping — so paste any YouTube video or livestream link. For the most reliable YouTube links, install the free Simple Clipping Helper extension in Chrome (one-time setup). Uploading a video file directly always works too. A longer video gives better clips. It takes a few minutes.
